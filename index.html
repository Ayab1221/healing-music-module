Healing Music: Matching Music to Patient Needs



Music can create connection, comfort, distraction, and support, but the most important part of the experience is the human connection.

In this module, you'll explore how a patient's condition, needs, preferences, and environment can influence the music selected during a session.

You'll practice:

  • Recognizing important patient and environmental considerations
  • Matching musical approaches to patient conditions
  • Identifying characteristics of rhythmic, arrhythmic, and improvised music
  • Applying these concepts to realistic patient situations
